🛒 KhataAI
AI Financial Intelligence for Small Merchants
KhataAI is an AI-powered financial intelligence platform built specifically for India's kirana stores and small retailers. It transforms traditional Khata-based bookkeeping into an intelligent digital financial system that helps merchants record transactions, manage customer credit, understand cash flow, and make data-driven business decisions.
🌟 The Problem
Millions of small merchants still depend on handwritten Khata books, manual calculations, and informal credit tracking to manage their businesses.
This creates several challenges:
- Difficult-to-maintain handwritten records
- Time-consuming manual transaction entry
- Limited visibility into customer credit
- Difficulty tracking outstanding payments
- No clear view of future cash availability
- Difficulty identifying potential cash-flow problems
- Traditional financial tools can be complex for small merchants
- Valuable financial information remains trapped in handwritten records
A merchant may know how much money is available today, but understanding how today's transactions, customer dues, expenses, and upcoming payments will affect the next few days is much harder.
💡 Our Solution
KhataAI converts everyday merchant activity into structured financial intelligence.
Instead of forcing merchants to adapt to complicated financial software, KhataAI works around familiar workflows such as:
Khata → Transactions → Customer Credit → Cash Flow → Insights
The platform combines AI, OCR, voice processing, customer intelligence, and cash-flow forecasting into a single merchant-focused system.
🚀 Key Features
📊 Intelligent Financial Dashboard
A centralized dashboard provides merchants with a real-time view of their business.
It includes:
- Today's sales
- Available cash
- Khata outstanding
- Customer dues
- 7-day cash-flow forecast
- Financial alerts
- Recent transactions
- AI Copilot
🎙️ Voice-to-Transaction
Merchants can record transactions naturally using their voice instead of manually filling forms.
For example, a merchant can say:
"Ramesh bought groceries worth ₹850 on credit."
KhataAI understands the statement and converts it into structured transaction information that can be reviewed and stored.
This makes transaction recording faster and more natural.
📷 Handwritten Khata OCR
Traditional handwritten Khata and Bahi records can be digitized using OCR.
Merchants can upload handwritten ledger images or documents, after which KhataAI:
Ledger → OCR → Text Extraction → AI Processing → Transaction Draft → Review → Database
This helps bridge the gap between traditional bookkeeping and digital financial management.
👥 Customer & Khata Intelligence
KhataAI provides a structured digital view of customer credit.
Merchants can track:
- Customer transactions
- Credit purchases
- Payments
- Outstanding balances
- Repayment history
- Customer-level Khata
This allows merchants to quickly understand who owes money and how much is outstanding.
🧠 Customer Entity Resolution
Customer names are often entered differently in real-world merchant records.
For example:
Ramesh, Ramesh Kumar, Ramesh Bhai, Ramesh Ji
KhataAI uses entity-resolution logic to identify such variations and associate them with the appropriate customer.
This reduces duplicate customer records and improves the accuracy of Khata tracking.
📈 7-Day Cash-Flow Forecasting
KhataAI provides short-term cash-flow forecasting based on the merchant's available financial information.
The forecasting system considers factors such as:
- Current cash
- Sales
- Expenses
- Customer payments
- Outstanding Khata
- Upcoming payments
The result is presented as a simple 7-day financial outlook so merchants can understand potential liquidity situations ahead of time.
🤖 KhataAI Copilot
KhataAI Copilot allows merchants to interact with their financial information using natural language.
Merchants can ask questions such as:
- Who has outstanding Khata?
- How much cash do I currently have?
- How much did I sell today?
- Who owes me money?
- What payments are coming up?
- What is my projected cash position?
- Show me recent transactions.
Instead of manually searching through multiple records, merchants can simply ask the system.
⚠️ Financial Alerts
KhataAI identifies important financial situations and surfaces them to the merchant.
Examples include:
- Low available cash
- Projected cash-flow gaps
- High outstanding customer dues
- Upcoming payments
- Significant financial changes
The goal is to move from simply recording financial data to understanding financial data
